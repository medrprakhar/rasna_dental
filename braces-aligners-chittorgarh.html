Braces and aligners in Chittorgarh

चित्तौड़गढ़ में टेढ़े दाँत सीधे करवाएँ: ब्रेसेस और क्लियर अलाइनर

Crooked or gapped teeth, front teeth that stick out, or a bite that doesn't meet properly can make teeth harder to clean and put uneven pressure on the jaw. Braces and aligners move the teeth slowly into a better position.

We treat children, teenagers and adults. Adults can get braces too, as long as the gums and bone are healthy.

Signs that you or your child might need braces

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between the teeth
  • Upper front teeth that stick out
  • Lower teeth that sit in front of the upper teeth
  • Teeth that don't meet when you bite
  • Thumb sucking or mouth breathing that has changed the shape of the teeth

For children, a first check around the age of 7 helps us spot problems early, even if treatment starts later.

Your options

Braces

Small brackets are fixed on the teeth and joined with a wire that we adjust every few weeks. Braces suit almost every case and keep working without you having to remember anything.

Clear aligners

A series of thin, transparent trays made for your teeth and changed every week or two. They're hard to notice and come out for eating and brushing, but they only work if you wear them about 22 hours a day.

Retainers

Once the teeth are straight, a retainer stops them drifting back. Wearing it as advised matters as much as the treatment itself.

How long does it take?

Most treatments take between one and two years, depending on how far the teeth need to move. Simple cases can be quicker. After the first examination and X-rays, we'll give you an estimate of the time and the number of visits.

Living with braces

  • Your teeth will feel tight for a few days after each adjustment. It settles on its own.
  • Brush carefully around the brackets after meals.
  • Avoid hard and sticky food like toffees, chewing gum and hard nuts, which can break the brackets.
  • Come for your adjustment visits on time, usually every four to six weeks.

Common questions

What is the right age for braces?

Braces work well from about 11 to 14, when most permanent teeth have come in, but adults can be treated too. A check-up around age 7 helps us plan the right time.

Are aligners better than braces?

Neither is better for everyone. Aligners are less noticeable and removable, but they need to be worn all day. Braces are fixed, so they keep working on their own. We'll suggest what suits your teeth and your routine.

Do braces hurt?

Teeth feel tight and a little sore for a few days after braces are fitted or adjusted. Most people get used to it within a week.

टेढ़े दाँतों का इलाज

टेढ़े-मेढ़े या आगे निकले दाँत, दाँतों के बीच गैप, या ऊपर-नीचे के दाँत ठीक से न मिलना, इन सबका इलाज ब्रेसेस या क्लियर अलाइनर से होता है। बच्चे हों या बड़े, मसूड़े स्वस्थ हों तो किसी भी उम्र में इलाज हो सकता है।

  • ब्रेसेस में दाँतों पर छोटे ब्रैकेट लगाकर तार से धीरे-धीरे दाँत सीधे किए जाते हैं।
  • क्लियर अलाइनर पारदर्शी ट्रे होती हैं, जो आसानी से दिखती नहीं। इन्हें दिन में लगभग 22 घंटे पहनना पड़ता है।
  • इलाज में आमतौर पर एक से दो साल लगते हैं।
  • इलाज के बाद रिटेनर पहनना ज़रूरी है, वरना दाँत फिर से खिसक सकते हैं।
