Cosmetic dentistry and smile design in Chittorgarh

चित्तौड़गढ़ में सुंदर मुस्कान के लिए कॉस्मेटिक डेंटिस्ट्री

If you find yourself hiding your smile because of stained, chipped or uneven teeth, there's usually a straightforward way to fix it. Cosmetic treatment can be a single visit for whitening, or a planned smile design that changes the shape and shade of several teeth.

We start by listening to what bothers you, then suggest the simplest way to get there.

What we can help with

Teeth whitening

Lightens teeth stained by tea, coffee, tobacco or age. Results depend on the type of staining, and we'll tell you what to expect before we start.

Veneers

Thin tooth-coloured shells fixed on the front of the teeth to change their shade, shape or size. Useful for gaps, chips and teeth that don't respond to whitening.

Smile design

For bigger changes, we plan the shape, length and shade of several teeth together so the result suits your face. It may combine whitening, veneers, caps or braces.

How we plan it

  1. 1 ConsultationWe look at your teeth, gums and bite, and talk about what you'd like to change.
  2. 2 Health firstAny cavities or gum problems are treated before cosmetic work, so the result lasts.
  3. 3 Plan and shadeWe agree on the treatment, the number of visits and the shade with you before we begin.

Keeping your new smile

  • Cut down on tea, coffee and tobacco, which stain teeth again over time
  • Keep up regular cleaning and check-ups
  • If you grind your teeth, ask us about a night guard to protect veneers and caps

Common questions

Does whitening damage the teeth?

Whitening done under a dentist's supervision is safe for healthy teeth. Some people feel sensitivity for a few days, which settles on its own. Whitening doesn't change the colour of caps, fillings or veneers.

Will my teeth look fake?

We aim for a result that suits your face and looks like your own teeth, and we agree on the shape and shade with you before starting.

How long do veneers last?

With good care, veneers usually last many years. Avoid biting very hard things with your front teeth.
