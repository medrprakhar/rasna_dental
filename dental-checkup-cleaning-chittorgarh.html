Dental check-ups and teeth cleaning in Chittorgarh

चित्तौड़गढ़ में दाँतों की जाँच और सफ़ाई

Most dental problems start small and quietly. A cavity that could have been a simple filling turns into a root canal, and bleeding gums turn into loose teeth. A check-up and cleaning every six months catches these early, when treatment is quicker and simpler.

What happens at a check-up

  • We check every tooth for cavities, cracks and worn fillings
  • We look at your gums for bleeding, swelling and pockets
  • We check the tongue, cheeks and palate for any unusual patches or ulcers
  • If needed, we take an X-ray to see between the teeth and below the gums

The check of the tongue and cheeks is also a simple screen for early signs of oral cancer. It matters most for anyone who uses tobacco, gutka or supari.

Scaling and polishing

Even with good brushing, hard deposits called tartar build up along the gum line, especially behind the lower front teeth. Tartar can't be brushed off. It causes bleeding gums and bad breath, and over time it loosens the teeth. Scaling removes it with an ultrasonic scaler, and polishing smooths the surface so stains and plaque don't stick as easily.

Your gums may feel a little sensitive for a day or two after scaling. That passes quickly.

Protecting children's teeth

Fluoride application

A fluoride gel or varnish painted on the teeth makes the enamel stronger and more resistant to cavities.

Pit and fissure sealants

A thin protective coating on the chewing surfaces of the back teeth, where food gets stuck and cavities often start.

Habits that keep teeth healthy

  • Brush twice a day for two minutes with a fluoride toothpaste
  • Clean between your teeth with floss or an interdental brush
  • Cut down on sweets and sugary drinks between meals
  • Avoid tobacco, gutka and supari
  • Come for a check-up every six months, even when nothing hurts

Common questions

Does scaling make teeth loose or weak?

No. Scaling removes tartar that was already pushing the gums away. Teeth can feel a little different afterwards because the spaces the tartar was filling are now clean, but scaling itself doesn't weaken teeth.

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

Bleeding gums usually mean the gums are inflamed from plaque and tartar. Keep brushing, and get your teeth cleaned. The bleeding usually settles once the tartar is gone.

How often should children see a dentist?

Every six months, starting from when the first teeth come in.

हर छह महीने में जाँच क्यों ज़रूरी है

दाँतों की ज़्यादातर बीमारियाँ धीरे-धीरे और बिना दर्द के शुरू होती हैं। छोटी कैविटी समय पर पकड़ में आ जाए तो साधारण फ़िलिंग से काम चल जाता है, वरना बाद में RCT तक की नौबत आ जाती है। इसलिए हर छह महीने में जाँच करवाएँ, चाहे दर्द हो या न हो।

  • ब्रश करने से दाँतों पर जमा पत्थर (टार्टर) नहीं हटता। इसे स्केलिंग से साफ़ किया जाता है।
  • मसूड़ों से ख़ून आना या मुँह से बदबू आना अक्सर टार्टर की वजह से होता है।
  • स्केलिंग से दाँत ढीले या कमज़ोर नहीं होते।
  • बच्चों के दाँतों को कीड़े से बचाने के लिए फ़्लोराइड और सीलेंट लगाया जा सकता है।

तंबाकू, गुटखा या सुपारी खाते हैं, तो नियमित जाँच और भी ज़रूरी है। मुँह में कोई सफ़ेद या लाल धब्बा या न भरने वाला छाला दिखे, तो तुरंत दिखाएँ।
