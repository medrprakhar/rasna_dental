Dentures in Chittorgarh

चित्तौड़गढ़ में बत्तीसी (डेंचर)

Dentures replace missing teeth with a set you can take out. They bring back chewing, clear speech and the shape of the face, which often sinks in when many teeth are lost.

We make full dentures, partial dentures, and dentures held firmly on implants, and we adjust and repair old dentures too.

Types of dentures

Full dentures

A complete set of teeth for the upper jaw, the lower jaw or both, when all the teeth in that jaw are missing.

Partial dentures

Replace a few missing teeth and clip onto the remaining natural teeth. They also stop the other teeth from drifting into the gaps.

Implant-supported dentures

Two to four implants hold the denture in place, so it doesn't slip while eating or talking. Very helpful for loose lower dentures.

Repairs and relining

Cracked dentures, broken teeth on the denture, or dentures that have become loose as the gums change shape over time.

What happens, step by step

  1. 1 ExaminationWe check the gums, the remaining teeth and the bone, and treat any problem that needs attention first.
  2. 2 MeasurementsWe take moulds of your mouth and record how your jaws meet.
  3. 3 TrialYou try the teeth set in wax, so you can see the look and we can check the bite before the final denture is made.
  4. 4 FittingThe finished denture is fitted and adjusted, and we show you how to wear and clean it.
  5. 5 AdjustmentsSmall sore spots are common in the first weeks. We adjust the denture at follow-up visits until it's comfortable.

Getting used to new dentures

  • Start with soft food cut into small pieces, and chew on both sides at once.
  • Speaking may feel different for a few days. Reading aloud helps.
  • Take the dentures out at night to let the gums rest, and keep them in water.
  • Clean them every day with a brush and plain soap or denture cleaner, not toothpaste, which scratches them.
  • Come for a check-up once a year. Gums change shape over time, and a loose denture can cause sores.

Common questions

How long does it take to make dentures?

Usually a few visits over two to three weeks, from the first measurements to the final fitting. Old teeth may need to be removed and healed first.

My denture keeps slipping. What can I do?

A loose denture can often be relined to fit better. If it still slips, especially the lower one, two implants can hold it firmly in place.

Can I sleep with my dentures in?

It's better to take them out at night. This lets the gums rest and lowers the risk of infection.

Denture or implant: which is better?

Implants are fixed and feel closer to natural teeth. Dentures are removable and don't need surgery. We'll explain the options for your mouth, including combining the two.

बत्तीसी (डेंचर) के बारे में

बत्तीसी निकले हुए दाँतों की जगह लगाया जाने वाला ऐसा सेट है जिसे निकाला और लगाया जा सकता है। इससे खाना चबाना, साफ़ बोलना और चेहरे का भराव वापस आता है।

  • सारे दाँत न हों तो पूरी बत्तीसी, और कुछ दाँत न हों तो आंशिक (पार्शियल) डेंचर बनता है।
  • ढीली बत्तीसी को इम्प्लांट से कसकर टिकाया जा सकता है।
  • पुरानी बत्तीसी की मरम्मत और ढीली बत्तीसी को फिर से फ़िट करना भी होता है।
  • शुरू में कुछ दिन नरम खाना खाएँ। कहीं चुभे तो बताएँ, हम उसे ठीक कर देंगे।
  • रात को बत्तीसी निकालकर पानी में रखें और रोज़ ब्रश से साफ़ करें।

बत्तीसी बनवाने में आमतौर पर दो-तीन हफ़्तों में कुछ विज़िट लगती हैं। साल में एक बार जाँच ज़रूर करवाएँ।
