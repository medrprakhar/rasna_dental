Gum treatment in Chittorgarh

चित्तौड़गढ़ में मसूड़ों का इलाज (पायरिया)

Bleeding gums are so common that many people think it's normal. It isn't. It's the first sign of gum disease, and if it's left alone, the gum and bone holding the teeth slowly wear away until the teeth become loose.

Caught early, gum disease is simple to treat. Even in later stages, treatment can stop it from getting worse and help you keep your teeth.

Signs of gum disease

  • Gums bleed when you brush or eat something hard
  • Gums look red, swollen or puffy
  • Bad breath or a bad taste that doesn't go away
  • Gums pulling back, making teeth look longer
  • Gaps opening up between teeth, or teeth feeling loose
  • Pus between the teeth and gums

Diabetes, smoking, tobacco and gutka make gum disease worse and faster. Gum problems can also make blood sugar harder to control, so treating the gums helps both.

How we treat it

Scaling

Removes the tartar above and just below the gum line. For early gum disease, this with good brushing is often enough.

Deep cleaning (root planing)

When pockets have formed under the gum, we clean and smooth the root surfaces below the gum line, under local anaesthesia, so the gum can heal back against the tooth.

Medicines and mouthwash

A short course of medicine or a medicated mouthwash may be added for infection, alongside the cleaning, never instead of it.

Advanced cases

If deep pockets remain after cleaning, a minor gum procedure may be needed. We'll explain this clearly if your case needs it.

What happens, step by step

  1. 1 Gum checkWe measure the pockets around each tooth and take X-rays to see how much bone support is left.
  2. 2 CleaningScaling, and deep cleaning where needed, usually done over one or more visits.
  3. 3 Home careWe show you how to brush and clean between your teeth properly. This matters as much as the cleaning at the clinic.
  4. 4 ReviewAfter a few weeks, we check how the gums have healed and plan regular maintenance visits.

Common questions

Should I stop brushing if my gums bleed?

No. Keep brushing gently but thoroughly. Bleeding comes from inflamed gums, and stopping brushing makes it worse. Get your teeth cleaned so the cause is removed.

Can loose teeth become firm again?

If the looseness is from swelling, teeth often firm up once the gums heal. Bone that has already been lost doesn't fully grow back, but treatment stops further loss and helps you keep the teeth longer.

Is pyorrhoea the same as gum disease?

Yes. Pyorrhoea is the common name for advanced gum disease, where there's pus, bone loss and loose teeth.

How often should I come after gum treatment?

Usually every three to six months, depending on how severe it was. Regular cleaning keeps it from coming back.

मसूड़ों से ख़ून आना सामान्य नहीं है

ब्रश करते समय मसूड़ों से ख़ून आना मसूड़ों की बीमारी का पहला संकेत है। इसे अनदेखा करें तो धीरे-धीरे दाँतों को पकड़ने वाली हड्डी घटने लगती है और दाँत हिलने लगते हैं। इसी को आम भाषा में पायरिया कहते हैं।

  • मसूड़ों से ख़ून, सूजन, मुँह से बदबू या दाँत हिलना, ये सब इसके लक्षण हैं।
  • शुरुआत में स्केलिंग और सही ब्रशिंग से ही ठीक हो जाता है।
  • ज़्यादा बढ़ गया हो तो मसूड़ों के नीचे की गहरी सफ़ाई (डीप क्लीनिंग) की जाती है।
  • ख़ून आए तो ब्रश करना बंद न करें। आराम से, पर अच्छी तरह ब्रश करते रहें।
  • डायबिटीज़, धूम्रपान, तंबाकू और गुटखा से मसूड़ों की बीमारी तेज़ी से बढ़ती है।

जितनी जल्दी इलाज शुरू हो, उतना अच्छा। इलाज के बाद हर तीन से छह महीने में सफ़ाई करवाते रहें, ताकि बीमारी दोबारा न लौटे।
