Children's dentistry in Chittorgarh

चित्तौड़गढ़ में बच्चों के दाँतों का इलाज

Milk teeth fall out, so many parents think a cavity in them doesn't matter. But milk teeth hold the space for the permanent teeth, help the child chew and speak, and an infected milk tooth can hurt as much as any other.

We take time with children, explain each step in simple words, and keep the first visits light so they don't grow up afraid of the dentist.

When to bring your child

  • For a first check-up once the first teeth have come in, or by the first birthday
  • Every six months after that
  • If you see brown or black spots, holes, or chalky white patches on the teeth
  • If the child complains of pain, won't chew on one side, or has swelling on the gum or face
  • If permanent teeth are coming in behind milk teeth that haven't fallen out

Treatments for children

Check-ups and cleaning

A gentle look at the teeth and gums, cleaning if needed, and tips for brushing at home.

Fillings

Small cavities in milk and permanent teeth filled with tooth-coloured material.

Root canal for milk teeth

When decay reaches the nerve of a milk tooth, a simpler root canal (pulpectomy) can save it until it's time to fall out naturally.

Fluoride and sealants

Fluoride makes enamel stronger, and sealants cover the deep grooves of back teeth where cavities usually start.

Space maintainers

If a milk tooth is lost too early, a small appliance keeps the space open for the permanent tooth.

Removing milk teeth

When a milk tooth is too damaged to save, or isn't falling out and is blocking the new tooth.

Simple habits that protect children's teeth

  • Brush twice a day. Use a rice-grain smear of fluoride toothpaste under 3, and a pea-sized amount after that.
  • Help with brushing until the child is about 7 or 8.
  • Don't put the baby to sleep with a milk bottle.
  • Keep sweets, biscuits and chocolates to mealtimes rather than all through the day.
  • Thumb-sucking beyond 4 or 5 years can affect how the teeth grow. Mention it at the check-up.

Common questions

Why treat milk teeth if they'll fall out anyway?

Milk teeth hold space for permanent teeth and help with chewing and speech. An untreated cavity can cause pain, infection and abscess, and an early loss can make the permanent teeth come in crooked.

At what age should my child first see a dentist?

Around the first birthday, or when the first teeth come in. Early visits are short and help the child get comfortable.

My child is scared. What should I do?

Tell us before the visit. We start slowly, show and explain the instruments, and keep the first visit simple. Avoid using the dentist as a threat at home.

When do children start losing milk teeth?

Usually around 6 years, starting with the lower front teeth. The last milk teeth fall out around 11 to 12 years.

दूध के दाँतों का ध्यान क्यों ज़रूरी है

दूध के दाँत गिर जाते हैं, इसलिए कई माता-पिता इनमें कीड़ा लगने को गंभीरता से नहीं लेते। लेकिन यही दाँत पक्के दाँतों के लिए जगह बनाए रखते हैं, और इनमें इन्फ़ेक्शन हो तो बच्चे को उतना ही दर्द होता है।

  • पहली जाँच पहले दाँत आने पर या पहले जन्मदिन तक करवाएँ, फिर हर छह महीने में।
  • दाँतों पर भूरे-काले धब्बे या गड्ढे दिखें, तो जल्दी दिखाएँ।
  • दूध के दाँत की नस तक कीड़ा पहुँच जाए तो उसका भी रूट कैनाल होता है।
  • फ़्लोराइड और सीलेंट से दाँतों को कीड़े से बचाया जा सकता है।
  • बच्चे को दूध की बोतल मुँह में लगाकर न सुलाएँ।

बच्चा डरता हो तो पहले बता दें। हम धीरे-धीरे, समझाकर इलाज करते हैं, ताकि बच्चे के मन में डेंटिस्ट का डर न बैठे।
