Privacy policy

Rasna Dental Care, Chittorgarh · Last updated 11 November 2026

Who we are

This website, rasnadental.com, is run by Rasna Dental Care, a dental clinic in Chittorgarh, Rajasthan. This page explains what information we collect through the website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send an appointment request through the form on this website, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your preferred date
  • Any message you write about your dental concern

We ask for your consent on the form before you send it. We don't ask for payment details or identity documents on this website.

How we use it

We use these details only to contact you about your appointment and your enquiry. We don't sell your details, and we don't share them with anyone for advertising.

Services that help us run the website

Appointment requests are delivered to our clinic email by a form service, FormSubmit. The website also loads a Google Map, and fonts and icons from outside providers. Like any website you visit, these providers may see technical details such as your IP address and browser type.

If you tap a WhatsApp, phone or Google Maps link, you leave this website, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies

This website doesn't set its own tracking or advertising cookies.

Google reviews and our reply tool

Our clinic uses a private, in-house tool called Rasna Review Bot to read the public reviews on our own Google Business Profile and to post the clinic's replies. The tool is used only by the clinic.

  • It accesses only our own Business Profile and the reviews on it, which are already public on Google.
  • It may use an automated writing service to help draft a reply. Replies are posted under the supervision of the clinic's doctors.
  • We never mention or confirm anyone's treatment in a reply.
  • We don't sell this information or use it for advertising, and we don't share it with anyone except as needed to post our replies.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep your details

Appointment requests stay in our clinic email for as long as we need them to arrange your visit and for our records. Clinical records made during treatment are kept at the clinic, separately from this website.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details you sent through this website, or withdraw your consent, by contacting us. We'll act on your request within a reasonable time.

Children

A parent or guardian should send appointment requests for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page with a new date.
