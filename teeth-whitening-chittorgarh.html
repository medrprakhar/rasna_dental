Teeth whitening in Chittorgarh

चित्तौड़गढ़ में दाँतों को सफ़ेद करना (टीथ व्हाइटनिंग)

Teeth slowly darken over the years from tea, coffee, tobacco, paan and simply age. Whitening lightens the shade of your natural teeth without drilling or covering them.

Before whitening, we check whether the stain is on the surface, which cleaning can remove, or inside the tooth, which needs whitening. Sometimes a simple scaling and polishing is all you need.

Who whitening is for

  • Yellowing from tea, coffee, tobacco or age
  • Healthy teeth and gums, with no untreated cavities
  • Adults who want a brighter shade without changing the shape of their teeth

When whitening won't work well

Whitening only changes natural teeth. Fillings, caps and veneers stay the same colour, so they may need replacing afterwards to match. Grey or deep stains from childhood medicines, fluorosis spots, or a single tooth that has turned dark after an injury often don't respond well to whitening. For these, veneers or a cap may give a better result, and we'll tell you honestly which suits your teeth.

What happens, step by step

  1. 1 Check-upWe look for cavities, gum problems and sensitive areas, and note your current shade.
  2. 2 CleaningScaling and polishing removes surface stains and tartar, so the whitening gel works evenly.
  3. 3 WhiteningThe gums are protected, and a whitening gel is applied to the teeth at the clinic.
  4. 4 AftercareWe tell you what to avoid for the next few days and how to keep the result longer.

Keeping your teeth white

  • For two days after whitening, avoid tea, coffee, cola, turmeric-heavy food and tobacco.
  • Rinse with water after tea or coffee.
  • Brush twice a day and come for scaling every six months.
  • Stopping tobacco and paan makes the biggest difference.

Common questions

Does whitening damage the enamel?

Whitening done under a dentist's supervision is safe for healthy teeth. It doesn't thin the enamel. Some people get sensitivity for a few days, which settles on its own.

How long does whitening last?

It depends on your habits. With less tea, coffee and tobacco, the result can last a long time. A touch-up may be needed later.

Will my caps and fillings also turn white?

No. Whitening works only on natural teeth. Visible fillings or caps may need replacing afterwards to match the new shade.

Are whitening toothpastes enough?

They can remove some surface stains but don't change the natural shade of the tooth. Clinic whitening does.

टीथ व्हाइटनिंग के बारे में

चाय, कॉफ़ी, तंबाकू, पान और उम्र के साथ दाँत धीरे-धीरे पीले पड़ जाते हैं। व्हाइटनिंग से बिना दाँत घिसे उनका रंग हल्का किया जाता है।

  • पहले जाँच होती है कि दाग़ ऊपरी सतह पर है या दाँत के अंदर।
  • कई बार सिर्फ़ स्केलिंग और पॉलिशिंग से ही दाँत साफ़ दिखने लगते हैं।
  • व्हाइटनिंग सिर्फ़ असली दाँतों पर असर करती है, फ़िलिंग या कैप पर नहीं।
  • कुछ दिन हल्की झनझनाहट हो सकती है, जो अपने-आप ठीक हो जाती है।
  • व्हाइटनिंग के बाद दो दिन चाय, कॉफ़ी और तंबाकू से बचें।

बचपन की दवाओं के गहरे दाग़ या चोट के बाद काला पड़ा दाँत व्हाइटनिंग से ठीक से सफ़ेद नहीं होता। ऐसे में विनियर या कैप बेहतर रहता है। जाँच के बाद हम आपको साफ़-साफ़ बताएँगे।
